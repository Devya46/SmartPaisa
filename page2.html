About Forex Market Trading Mastery

Master the fundamentals and advanced concepts required to understand the forex market with greater clarity. Build a structured approach to technical analysis, market behaviour and trading decisions through a professional learning path.

Why this course? Designed by institutional traders, this curriculum bridges the gap between academic theory and real-world application. You'll learn to read price action like a professional, manage risk effectively, and develop a trading system that fits your personality.

This course is suitable for beginners who want a solid foundation, as well as intermediate traders looking to refine their edge. All concepts are taught in plain English with live examples and practical exercises.

Your Learning Roadmap

A step-by-step path to forex mastery, from foundation to advanced execution. Each phase builds on the previous one, ensuring a smooth learning curve.

Phase 1: Foundation

Market structure, currency pairs, pips & lots, trading sessions, and broker types.

Phase 2: Technical Analysis

Trends, support/resistance, indicators (MACD, RSI, Bollinger Bands), and chart patterns.

Phase 3: Trading Strategies

Scalping, day trading, swing trading, position sizing, and strategy backtesting.

Phase 4: Risk & Psychology

Risk management, emotional control, trade journaling, and building discipline.

Phase 5: Live Execution

Demo trading, transitioning to a live account, and performance review.

Phase 6: Mastery

Advanced setups, algorithmic trading basics, and portfolio building.

Estimated timeline: 12 weeks (self-paced). You can accelerate or take more time depending on your schedule.

Course Modules

10 comprehensive modules covering everything from the basics to advanced trading techniques.

  • Module 1: Forex Market Overview & Mechanics — How the market works, major players, and trading sessions.
  • Module 2: Candlestick Patterns & Price Action — Single and multi-candle patterns, market context.
  • Module 3: Indicators & Oscillators — MACD, RSI, Stochastic, Bollinger Bands, and volume.
  • Module 4: Support, Resistance & Trendlines — Drawing techniques, polarity, and breakouts.
  • Module 5: Advanced Chart Patterns — Head & Shoulders, Flags, Wedges, Triangles, and more.
  • Module 6: Trading Systems & Backtesting — Building a rule-based system, testing with historical data.
  • Module 7: Money Management & Position Sizing — Risk per trade, reward-to-risk ratio, and drawdown management.
  • Module 8: Trading Psychology & Discipline — Overcoming fear and greed, developing a winning mindset.
  • Module 9: Live Market Analysis & Execution — Applying your skills in real-time, trade entry and exit.
  • Module 10: Building a Trading Business — From strategy to consistency, scaling up, and automation.
